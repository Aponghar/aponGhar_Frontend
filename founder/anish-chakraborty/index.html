About Anish Chakraborty

Anish Chakraborty is an AI/ML Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, and technology entrepreneur based in Assam, India. With an educational background in Mathematics and extensive hands-on training in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Guwahati, Anish merges rigorous algorithmic fundamentals with practical product engineering.

His primary engineering focus encompasses full-stack platform development, database architecture, machine learning workflows, and data engineering. He created Aponghar to address fragmented accommodation discovery and property management across Northeast India, building the entire digital system from foundation to production.
